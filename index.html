Abstract Realism

Unconventional composition, creative exploration and a vivid palette. A bridge between the landscape you see and the landscape you feel.

Welcome to my studio

I am an award-winning Canadian artist with paintings in collections around the world.

In my work I explore the Canadian landscape and emphasize nature's similarities and patterns to create paintings using strong colour, discrete shapes and visual rhythms.

I hope you enjoy my art for both its abstract and realistic qualities.
