About the Studies

These small works are where I experiment, play, and take risks. Each study is a quick exploration of color, composition, or a fleeting moment in nature — a way to test ideas without the pressure of a larger canvas.

Studies are the seeds of my larger paintings. Sometimes a small piece stands on its own; other times, it sparks a whole new direction. They're intimate, spontaneous, and often the most honest work I create.

These pieces are all originals, created in my studio in Toronto. I hope you enjoy them as much as I enjoyed making them.
