About Mandy Budan

Biography

I'm a Canadian artist working mostly in acrylics. I paint abstract landscapes - rearranging the real elements into something unexpected. Nature is the starting point. I use bold color, distinct shapes, and rhythm to make paintings that work both as abstraction and as landscape.

Most of what I paint comes from Ontario. I spend time in provincial parks, conservation areas, and the quiet green spaces in between, chasing that particular combination of light and color that makes me want to pick up a brush.
