About Mandy Budan

I paint the Canadian landscape as memory, not photograph — abstract enough to invite your own interpretation, grounded enough to feel like home.

"Nature is the starting point. I use bold color, distinct shapes, and rhythm to make paintings that work both as abstraction and as landscape." - Mandy Budan

My Work

I'm a Canadian artist working mostly in acrylics on wood. I paint abstract landscapes — rearranging the real elements into something unexpected. Nature is the starting point. I use bold color, distinct shapes, and rhythm to make paintings that work both as abstraction and as landscape.

Where I Paint

Most of what I paint comes from Ontario. I spend time in provincial parks, conservation areas, and the quiet green spaces in between, chasing that particular combination of light and color that makes me want to pick up a brush. Georgian Bay, Muskoka, Haliburton, the Ottawa Valley — these are my muses.

Background

My work is in private and corporate collections in Australia, Canada, China, the U.K. and the U.S. I've been juried into international exhibitions and have received recognition from ISAP, Oshawa Art Association, Pine Ridge Arts, and Canadian Brushstrokes Magazine.
