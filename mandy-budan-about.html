About Mandy Budan

Biography

Mandy Budan is a Canadian abstract landscape painter based in Toronto, Ontario. Her work explores the intersection of memory, emotion, and the natural world through bold color and expressive brushwork.

With a background in fine arts from the University of Toronto, Mandy has been creating abstract landscapes for over 20 years. Her paintings capture the essence of Canadian scenery — from the rocky shores of Georgian Bay to the rolling hills of Muskoka.
