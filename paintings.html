About the Work

These paintings are my love letter to the Canadian landscape — the rugged shores of Georgian Bay, the quiet forests of Muskoka, the rolling hills of the Ottawa Valley. Each piece begins with a memory, a feeling, a moment of wonder in nature that I want to hold onto and share.

I work in acrylic on wood, building up layers of color and texture through an intuitive process that balances spontaneity with intention. The result is a body of work that is both energetic and meditative — abstract enough to invite your own interpretation, grounded enough to feel like home.

Every painting here is an original, created by my hands in my studio in Toronto. Thank you for being here, and I hope you find something that speaks to you.
