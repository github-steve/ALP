FINE ART PRINTS, ORIGINAL PAINTED STUDIES AND ITEMS WITH MY IMAGES

I offer fine art digital prints (giclee prints) of many of my abstract landscape paintings. I have a variety of fine art prints available including signed prints, canvas prints, note cards, and mounted prints as well as puzzles, journals, textiles and housewares at a number of online locations.

  • budanart.com
    budanArt is my online store and is where I list my original paintings, as well as signed fine art landscape prints and note cards.
  • dailypaintworks.com
    DailyPaintworks is an online auction and listing site. I auction my small abstract landscape studies here. I usually list my studies Saturday mornings.
  • fineartamerica.com and pixels.com
    Fine Art America is one of the biggest sites for fine art prints on all sorts of materials. I list a variety of landscapes and florals here - prints on wood, metal, canvas and more. I also have abstract landscape note cards and textiles including tote bags, towels and phone cases.
  • StumpCraft Puzzles
    Canadian fine art laser-cut puzzles.
  • Art and Fable Puzzle Company
    Fine art puzzles.
  • Dia Noche Designs
    Dia Noche carries variety of fine art printed textiles including shower curtains, blankets, pillows and more!
  • etsy.com
    One of the biggest handmade indie sites around; I have been listing my work here since 2005! :) I offer signed abstract landscape prints and note cards.
